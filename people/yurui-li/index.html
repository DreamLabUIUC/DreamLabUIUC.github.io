Ph.D. Student in Informatics · University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign · DREAM Lab

I am a Ph.D. student in Informatics at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, advised by Prof. Haohan Wang. My research focuses on developing computational methods based on deep learning and large language models to study cellular interactions, regulatory processes, and the biological mechanisms underlying them.

I aim to advance AI-driven approaches that enable deeper insights into complex cellular systems and facilitate biological discovery.

Research

  • Context-aware gene regulatory mechanism discovery with LLMs

    2026 – present

    An LLM-based framework that identifies context-dependent gene regulatory mechanisms by combining a biological knowledge graph of experimentally supported interactions with context-specific molecular evidence, using evidence-guided reasoning.

  • AI virtual cells and spatial perturbation modeling

    2026 – present

    A review of mathematical and AI-based virtual cell models, and a probabilistic spatial perturbation framework that predicts how perturbing a cell affects its neighbors, accounting for cellular heterogeneity and the local microenvironment.

  • Cell–cell communication and cell-specific gene regulation (HEISN)

    2025

    A hierarchical graph neural network that jointly infers intercellular signaling and cell-specific gene regulatory networks from spatial transcriptomics, applied to oral squamous cell carcinoma and Alzheimer's disease data.

  • Spatially varying gene regulatory networks (SVGRN)

    2024

    A two-stage deep learning framework combining structural equation modeling with conditional variational autoencoders to infer cell- and spot-specific gene regulatory networks that vary with spatial context.
