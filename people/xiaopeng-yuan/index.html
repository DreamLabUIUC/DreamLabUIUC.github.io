About

Xiaopeng Yuan is a first-year PhD student at the UIUC School of Information Sciences, advised by Haohan Wang. He studies LLM reasoning, test-time training, and long-context learning to make language models more capable and reliable on complex tasks. He hopes to build AI tools that people find genuinely useful and that benefit a wide range of users.

Research interests

  • LLM reasoning
  • Test-time training
  • Long-context learning
  • Reasoning in multi-agent systems
