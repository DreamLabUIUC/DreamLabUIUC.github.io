About

Peng Kuang is a PhD student at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, working in Prof. Haohan Wang’s DREAM Lab.

He studies how to develop effective and efficient supervision for evolving AI systems, and how those systems can learn and adapt from feedback. His work aims to make AI more reliable and capable while keeping the cost of supervision practical and scalable as systems take on increasingly complex tasks.

Research interests:

  • LLM agents
  • AI supervision
  • Efficiency
