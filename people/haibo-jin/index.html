About

Haibo Jin is a PhD student at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, advised by Prof. Haohan Wang in the DREAM Lab. His research focuses on building reliable, efficient, and scalable agentic AI systems through reusable primitives and harness engineering. His broader interests include AI safety and generative engine optimization (GEO), with an emphasis on improving the trustworthiness and effectiveness of AI systems in real-world applications.

Research interests

Highlight

Agent Primitives: Reusable Latent Building Blocks for Multi-Agent Systems
Haibo Jin, Peng Kuang, Ye Yu, Xiaopeng Yuan, Haohan Wang. International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML), 2026.

The paper shows how complex multi-agent systems can be built from a small set of reusable primitives, improving efficiency, scalability, and robustness while simplifying how agents collaborate.
