How to Test Whether Your Product Descriptions Influence AI Shopping Recommendations

A step-by-step guide, using our CORE method and the ProductBench benchmark

Published June 13, 2026

Want to test whether your product descriptions influence AI shopping recommendations? This is exactly the question our paper studies. Below is a step-by-step way to measure it, the definitions you need, a results table, and an FAQ. Method and benchmark come from our paper, CORE (arXiv:2602.03608).

Key definitions

  • AI shopping recommendation — a short, ranked set of products an LLM-based search system recommends in its generated answer.
  • Retrieved content — the product text (descriptions, reviews) the search engine pulls in before the LLM writes its recommendation.
  • Optimization content — text added to the retrieved content to influence the ranking; CORE studies three types (string-based, reasoning-based, review-based).
  • Promotion Success Rate (PSR) — how often the target product is promoted into the top-K of the generated ranking.
  • ProductBench — our benchmark for this exact measurement (defined below).

How to test it, step by step

  1. Define the target and query. Pick the target product and the shopping query you want to test (e.g. “best wireless earbuds for running”).
  2. Collect a baseline. Record the baseline recommendation set — for example, the top-10 from a real shopping interface, the way ProductBench uses Amazon’s top-10.
  3. Vary the retrieved content. Change the product description / retrieved content using one of the three optimization-content types (string-based, reasoning-based, or review-based).
  4. Query search-enabled LLMs. Ask the shopping query to search-enabled LLMs and record where your product appears in the generated recommendations.
  5. Measure promotion. Compute Promotion Success Rate @Top-1/3/5, comparing with vs without your change, across multiple models.

FAQ

Do I need access to the LLM to test this? No — it is a black-box test: you only change retrieved content and observe the generated recommendations.

What should I measure? Promotion Success Rate @Top-1/3/5 — whether your product is promoted into the top of the generated ranking, with vs without the change.

Is there a ready-made benchmark? Yes — ProductBench (15 categories × 200 products, with Amazon top-10 recommendations).

Which LLMs did the paper test? GPT-4o, Gemini-2.5, Claude-4, and Grok-3.
